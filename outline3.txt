Reflection (Stage 3)
1) What changed between your Stage 1 outline and your final implementation? Why did you change it?
I kept the same major structure from the outline, but I grouped the input and buttons into a clean controls area so users can follow steps more easily. I also added clearer section headings and helper text so the page is easier to use during repeated test runs.

2) What was the hardest part to implement correctly, and why?
The hardest part was handling user input from prompt() safely because users can cancel or type invalid values. I solved this by validating each entered item cost and repeating the prompt until a valid number is provided.

3) Describe one mistake you made and how you fixed it.
One mistake was allowing old scanned items to stay in the list when scanning a new transaction. I fixed it by clearing the list and resetting cashRegister totals at the start of scan() before collecting new item prices.
